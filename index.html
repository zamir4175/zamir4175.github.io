💡 What is esphole?

esphole turns your ESP32-S3 hardware into a powerful network filter. It acts as a gatekeeper for your home internet. When you browse the web, your devices ask the esphole to find website addresses. The system checks these requests against a list of known trackers and advertisements. If a request matches a blocked entry, the system stops it immediately. This blocks ads before they reach your screens.

The system works silently in the background. It uses encrypted connections to keep your browsing habits private. You can manage your settings through a simple web page that works on both light and dark modes.

🚀 Getting Started

Setting up your device takes only a few minutes. Follow these instructions to prepare your hardware.

  1. Prepare your Hardware: Ensure you have an ESP32-S3 development board. Connect the board to your Windows computer using a high-quality USB cable.
  2. Download the Software: Visit the official release page to get the necessary firmware files for your device.
  3. Installation: Open the provided installer tool on your Windows machine. Select your device from the list of available ports. Press the install button to flash the firmware to your ESP32-S3.
  4. Configure Wi-Fi: Once the installation finishes, the device will create a temporary Wi-Fi network. Connect your phone or computer to the "esphole-setup" network. A login page will appear. Enter your home Wi-Fi details so the device connects to your local internet.

⚙️ System Features

  • Ad Blocking: Automatically filters out common advertising domains.
  • Tracker Protection: Blocks scripts that follow your movement across different websites.
  • Encrypted Upstream: Uses DNS-over-TLS to hide your lookup requests from your service provider.
  • OTA Rollback: Keeps the system safe by reverting to a stable state if an update fails.
  • Web Interface: Use a clean web dashboard to view statistics and manage blocked lists. The interface automatically adjusts between light and dark themes based on your system preferences.

📝 Frequently Asked Questions

Will this slow down my internet? No. The ESP32-S3 processes these requests quickly. You should notice no difference in browsing speed.

What if the device loses power? The system has a fail-open design. If the hardware stops working, the network handles requests normally without filtering. Your internet will remain active.

Can I customize the block list? Yes. The web interface allows you to add or remove domains from the block list easily.
